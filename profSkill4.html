      WHAT IS LINKEDLN?
- Professional social networking platform
- Post a professional profile, network, research employers, and apply for positions
- Over 930 million users worldwide, 29 million in Canada

DO EMPLOYERS USE LINKDLN?
- 72% of employers recruit via Linkedln (2020 Jobvite Recruiter Nation Survey)
- Critical tool for career college students entering the job market

BUILDING A PROFESSIONAL PROFILE
- Professional photo, consistent across platforms 
- Strong headline and summary
- Highlight education, internships, projects, and work experience
- Personalized Linkedln URL
- Recommendations from mentors or employers
- Keep your profile updated regularly

FEATURING YOUR WORK
- Upload work samples: documents, videos, links, presentations
- Check for copy right or  confidentiality issues before posting
- Use 'Edit Profile -> Upload' to add samples

CONNECTING WITH CONTACTS
1. Personalize connection invitations
2. Import contacts from email or other social media
3. Treat your connections professionally: thank them maintain relationships

NETWORKING ON LINKEDLN
Search for College's alumni and industry professionals.
Request introductions through mutual contacts
Join and participate in relevant groups
Ask for advice, not a job - learn about industries and hiring trends

MAINTAINING RELATIONSHIP
- Send thank-you notes and updates
- Keep requests reasonable
- Give back share valuable articles or resources
- Periodically update contacts on your progress

FINDING CAREER & EMPLOYMENT INFORMATION
- Company search: learn about employees, stats, and posted positions
- Job listing: filter by location, company, experience level
- Industry information: join groups to learn trends and events

PUBLISHING ARTICLES ON LINKEDLN
Post articles to showcase professional knowledge and writing skills
Great way to demonstrate expertise
Check Linkedln Help Centre for tips and best practices

HOW CAN LINKEDLN

EMAIL ETIQUETTE
- Profiles are public, set privacy according to comfort level
- Keep your profile professional and positive
- ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) may scan your Linkedln presence

EFFECTIVE COLD CALLING EMAILING STRATEGIES
- Focus on internships, co-ops, and part-time opportunities
- Emphasize transferable skill gained in college programs
- Highlight projects, certifications, and volunteering 
- Seek guidance from career Centre for photos, profile review, and networking
